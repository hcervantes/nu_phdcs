### **Key Topics to Review**
1. **Doctoral Research Methodologies**
   - Qualitative vs. quantitative research in CS.
   - Case studies, experiments, and theoretical proofs.

2. **Theoretical Frameworks**
   - Automata theory, computational complexity, and formal methods.
   - Foundational papers or theories in CS (e.g., Turing’s work, Church-Turing thesis).

3. **Practical Applications**
   - How theoretical concepts are implemented in systems (e.g., compilers, distributed systems).
   - Industry vs. academic research approaches.

4. **Ph.D. Approaches in CS**
   - Interdisciplinary research (e.g., CS + biology, CS + ethics).
   - Emerging fields (e.g., quantum computing, AI ethics).
