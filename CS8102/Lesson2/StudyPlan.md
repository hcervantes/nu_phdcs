### **Key Topics to Review**
1. **Theoretical Frameworks in CS**
   - **Automata Theory** (Maurer 2021): Finite automata, pushdown automata, and their real-world applications.
   - **Abstraction/Representation (AR) Theory** (Fletcher 2018): Novel forms of computation and conceptualizing software systems.
   - **Conscious Turing Machine (CTM)** (Blum & Blum 2022): Computational principles of consciousness.

2. **Practical Applications of Theory**
   - **Case studies** from Maurer (2021) on linking theory to real-world systems.
   - **Software Science & Computation Structures** (Optional: [Foundations of Software Science](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-99253-8)): Theory applied to program analysis and verification.

3. **Doctoral Research Methodologies**
   - **Theoretical Framework Checklist** (CTL Brochure): Ensure your Assignment 2 includes a strong theoretical foundation.
   - **Dissertation Center Resources**: Use for structuring research questions and problem statements.

4. **Comparative Analysis**
   - Compare **theoretical rigor** (e.g., AR theory, automata) with **practical feasibility** (e.g., industry applications).
   - Discuss **gaps** in current CS research (e.g., bridging theory and practice).
